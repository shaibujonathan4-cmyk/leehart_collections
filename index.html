Our Story

Crafted For Women Who Dress With Intention

Leehart Collections started with a simple frustration: it was hard to find modest wear that felt genuinely luxurious, not just modest as an afterthought. We set out to change that — sourcing embellished silk-blend abayas, structured leather bags, spinner luggage sets, and crystal-accented footwear that hold up to a closer look.

Every piece is inspected before it ships, and every order is handled personally — when you reach out, you're talking to our team directly, not a call centre.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you order

How do I place an order?

Browse the collections above, tap "Enquire" on anything you like, then send your enquiry — it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our team so we can confirm size, colour and delivery before payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently confirm orders and take payment directly over WhatsApp once your details are finalised. We'll share our bank transfer details or payment link at that stage.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are tracked and delivered within a few business days of confirmation. Exact timing depends on your location, and we'll confirm it with you directly.

Can I exchange or return an item?

Yes — we offer a 7-day, hassle-free exchange window from the day your order arrives, as long as the item is unused and in its original packaging.

Do you offer custom sizing?

For abayas, let us know your preferred length and fit when you enquire — we're happy to advise or adjust where possible.
