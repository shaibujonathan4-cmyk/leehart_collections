How this demo works
A short explainer for reviewing this preview.

Anything you add, edit or remove under Products or Site Content updates the live customer site immediately, in this browser. Customers browse products, open a product for details, and send an enquiry that opens pre-filled on WhatsApp — there's no payment step, since Leehart Collections currently takes orders by chat. In the final delivered version, this data is saved centrally instead of per-browser, so your changes show up for every visitor from any device.
